# Sales Presentation Design — Nurul Hikmah App

## Objective
Create a persuasive client-facing presentation for `Nurul Hikmah App` that helps decision-makers understand the product quickly and increases the chance of purchase.

The presentation must position the product as a long-term digital investment for schools, not just a website or a one-off IT project.

## Audience
- School owners or foundation leaders
- Principals
- Administrative decision-makers
- Mixed decision-making groups in school procurement or digital transformation meetings

## Core Message
`Nurul Hikmah App` is a complete school digital ecosystem that combines public presence, admissions, and administration in one solution.

Primary promise:

> Sekali investasi untuk fondasi digital sekolah jangka panjang.

This phrase should be expressed in professional sales language, avoiding overly aggressive claims like "beli 1 kali seumur hidup" while preserving the intended meaning.

## Presentation Tone
- Formal and professional
- Modern and technology-forward
- Islami and elegant
- Persuasive without sounding exaggerated

## Presentation Goals
1. Make the client feel that current manual or fragmented systems are inefficient.
2. Present `Nurul Hikmah App` as the integrated solution.
3. Emphasize strengths far more than weaknesses.
4. Explain the architecture in simple, non-technical language.
5. Build trust through clear structure, attractive design, and relevant visuals.

## Recommended Format
- Slide deck in a visual presentation format suitable for client meetings
- Approximately 10 to 12 slides
- Strong cover, problem framing, solution narrative, benefits, architecture, and closing CTA

## Slide Structure
1. Cover
2. Common school problems
3. Product overview / integrated solution
4. Main features
5. Key advantages
6. System architecture
7. Impact for the school
8. Manual process vs digital process comparison
9. Small limitations / implementation notes
10. Why now
11. Value proposition
12. Closing CTA

## Content Strategy

### Strengths
Strengths should dominate the deck and be framed as business, branding, and operational value:
- One integrated platform for multiple school needs
- Better school image and professionalism
- Easier admissions workflow
- Easier content and information management
- Faster service for parents and students
- Structured and secure data management
- Supports long-term growth
- Reduces repetitive manual work

### Weaknesses
Weaknesses should be brief and honest, but framed as manageable adoption realities:
- Initial adaptation is needed for the school team
- Results depend on consistent content and admin usage

Weaknesses must not undermine the buying decision or create the impression of product instability.

## Architecture Slide
The architecture must be shown in a simplified client-friendly flow:

`Pengunjung / Orang Tua / Calon Siswa`
-> `Website & PPDB Online`
-> `Backend Sistem`
-> `Database Sekolah`
-> `Dashboard Admin`

Supporting explanation should use non-technical Bahasa Indonesia:
- public users access information and admissions online
- the system processes requests safely in the background
- school data is stored in a structured database
- administrators manage content and operations from one dashboard

## Visual Direction
- Dominant colors: deep green, soft gold, white, modern gray
- Large headlines with persuasive wording
- Premium spacing with minimal clutter
- Blend of institutional visuals and modern digital UI
- Photos should support themes like school professionalism, Islamic education, digital learning, administration, and admissions

## Visual Assets
- Use internet-sourced images appropriate for education and modern school branding
- Prefer professional, high-quality, client-safe visuals
- Images should reinforce trust, growth, and modernization

## Deliverable
Create a polished presentation artifact that can be shown directly to clients and easily converted or adapted into PPT delivery format if needed.

## Out of Scope
- Detailed technical developer documentation
- Internal engineering architecture deep-dives
- Pricing strategy or contractual terms unless requested later

## Validation Checklist
- The deck is understandable by non-technical decision-makers
- The message clearly sells long-term value
- Advantages are much more prominent than drawbacks
- The architecture is simple and reassuring
- The visual style feels formal, modern, and Islami-elegant
